But I guess the first thing I want you to know is that I'm genuinely so grateful to have you as my best friend. Out of all the people we could have met and all the friendships we could have had, I'm really glad that somehow, we ended up becoming friends and I hope you know that our friendship means a lot to me, even if I don't always say it properly.

You're someone I can laugh with over the most ridiculous things ever. Sometimes we probably laugh at things that wouldn't even make sense to anyone else, and honestly, I think that's one of the best parts of having a best friend. It's having someone who understands your nonsense, someone you can be completely weird around, and someone who can turn an ordinary day into a really special memory without even trying. Those little moments might not seem like a big deal at the time, but they're the kind of things I end up appreciating so much.

I hope you never feel like you have to act differently around me just to be liked or accepted. You don't need to be interesting every second, you don't need to be happy all the time, and you definitely don't need to pretend that everything is fine when it isn't. You can have bad days, you can be annoyed, you can be quiet, and you can be your completely unfiltered idiotic self. You're still my best friend through all of that. Friendship isn't about being perfect or always knowing what to say. Sometimes it's just about being there, listening, laughing at stupid things, and making sure the other person knows they aren't alone.

And please, PLEASE, never forget how much you matter as a person. Not because of how you look, how well you do in school, how many people like you, or how much attention you get. You matter simply because you're YOU. There is so much more to you than the things you might judge yourself for, and I really hope that as you grow older, you learn to see more of the good things in yourself. I hope you become more confident in who you are, more comfortable with being yourself, and less worried about whether everyone else approves of you.

Now that you're fifteen, I hope this year brings you so many good things. I hope you make memories that you will still laugh about years from now. I hope you discover new things you love, try things you've always wanted to try, and give yourself the chance to experience things that make you genuinely happy. I hope you meet people who appreciate you, make friends who bring out the best in you, and find opportunities that help you grow into the person you want to become.

I also hope that when things don't go the way you planned, you remember that one difficult day doesn't define your entire life. You don't have to have everything figured out at fifteen. You don't need to know exactly who you're going to become, what your future will look like, or how everything is supposed to work out. We're still growing, learning, making mistakes, changing our minds, and figuring ourselves out. So please don't put too much pressure on yourself to have everything together. Take your time, enjoy being OLD and let yourself make mistakess, learn from themm, and try againnnn.

I hope you give yourself permission to rest when you're tired, to celebrate yourself when something goes well, and to be proud of even the little things you accomplish. Sometimes we get so focused on the things we haven't done yet that we forget to appreciate how far we've already come. I hope this year teaches you to be kinder to yourself and to recognise your own progress, even when it feels small.

And, obviously, I hope you get LOTS AND LOTS OF HAPPINESS THIS YEAR. Like, an unreasonable amount of happiness. I hope you have days where everything seems to go right, days filled with your favourite food, your favourite songs, funny conversations, unexpected surprises, and people who make you feel appreciated. I hope you laugh until you can't breathe, take a ridiculous number of pictures, and have so many little moments that make you think, 'Wait, life is actually pretty nice sometimes.'

I hope you achieve the goals you've been working towards, but I also hope you remember that your grades and achievements aren't the only things that make you valuable. You deserve to feel proud of yourself for trying, for learning, for being brave enough to keep going, and for all the effort that other people might not even notice.

Thank you for being someone I can share parts of my life with. I'm sure we haven't even made all the memories we're going to look back on someday, and I really hope there are so many more waiting for us.
